AranDocs Privacy Policy

Effective Date: June 2, 2026

Overview

AranDocs is an offline document scanner, PDF/image generator, and secure document vault. This Privacy Policy explains what information is stored locally on your device, why certain permissions are required, and how you can manage your data.

Information We Collect

AranDocs stores the following information locally on your device:

  • Scanned images and generated PDF files.
  • Document metadata, including file name, format, size, page count, and timestamps.
  • Application settings and preferences.
  • Optional vault configuration settings.

How We Use Your Information

The information stored by AranDocs is used solely to:

  • Scan and process documents.
  • Generate PDF and image files.
  • Manage, organize, and display saved documents.
  • Allow document export and sharing.
  • Protect vault content using device authentication when enabled.

Permissions

Camera
Used to capture document images during scanning.

Storage
Used to save, read, modify, and delete scanned documents, PDFs, and vault files stored on your device.

Internet
Required by certain platform components and libraries. AranDocs does not upload your documents or personal data to external servers.

Data Storage and Retention

All documents, images, metadata, and settings are stored locally on your device.

AranDocs does not provide cloud storage or automatic synchronization.

You may delete documents at any time using the application or by uninstalling the app.

Sharing

Sharing is always initiated by you through your device's native sharing options.

AranDocs does not share documents or personal information without your explicit action.

Security

AranDocs offers optional vault protection using your device's built-in security features, including biometrics, PIN, password, or pattern authentication.

Biometric information is managed entirely by your device's operating system. AranDocs does not collect, store, or access biometric data.

Analytics and Tracking

AranDocs does not use analytics services, advertising SDKs, tracking technologies, or telemetry systems.

No usage data is transmitted to external servers.

Children's Privacy

AranDocs is not directed toward children under the age of 13. The application does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any updates will be published on this page with a revised effective date.
